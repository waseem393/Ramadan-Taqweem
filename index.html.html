Powerful Ramadan Duas 🤲

🌙 Dua for Fasting

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي لَكَ صُمْتُ وَبِكَ آمَنْتُ وَعَلَيْكَ تَوَكَّلْتُ وَعَلَى رِزْقِكَ أَفْطَرْتُ

Meaning: O Allah, I fasted for You, I believe in You, I put my trust in You, and with Your provision I break my fast.

✨ Laylatul Qadr Dua

اللَّهُمَّ إِنَّكَ عَفُوٌّ تُحِبُّ الْعَفْوَ فَاعْفُ عَنِّي

Meaning: O Allah, You are Most Forgiving, and You love forgiveness, so forgive me.

💖 Dua for Mercy

رَبِّ اغْفِرْ وَارْحَمْ وَأَنْتَ خَيْرُ الرَّاحِمِينَ

Meaning: My Lord, forgive and have mercy, for You are the Best of the merciful.

🌟 Dua for Guidance

رَبَّنَا لَا تُزِغْ قُلُوبَنَا بَعْدَ إِذْ هَدَيْتَنَا

Meaning: Our Lord, do not let our hearts deviate after You have guided us.

Ramadhan Recipes

Balaleet

25 mins | 480 kcal | Serves 3

Ingredients : Roasted vermicelli, eggs, ghee, sugar, saffron, cardamom, rose water
  1. Roast vermicelli in ghee until golden brown.
  2. Add water and cook until soft.
  3. Mix in sugar, saffron, and cardamom.
  4. Cook until fragrant and fluffy.
  5. Beat eggs with a pinch of salt.
  6. Prepare a thin omelette separately.
  7. Place omelette over vermicelli and serve warm.

Chicken Majboos

60 mins | 650 kcal | Serves 6

Ingredients : Chicken pieces, basmati rice, baharat spice, onions, tomatoes, garlic, dried lime, raisins
  1. Sauté onions and garlic in oil.
  2. Add chicken and brown on all sides.
  3. Mix in tomatoes and baharat spices.
  4. Add dried lime and cook until chicken is tender.
  5. Add soaked basmati rice to the pot.
  6. Pour water and cook until rice is fluffy.
  7. Garnish with raisins and serve hot.

Shakshuka

30 mins | 350 kcal | Serves 4

Ingredients : Eggs, fresh tomatoes, bell peppers, onion, garlic, cumin, paprika, olive oil, feta cheese
  1. Heat olive oil in a pan.
  2. Sauté onions and bell peppers.
  3. Add garlic and cook briefly.
  4. Stir in tomatoes and spices.
  5. Simmer until the sauce thickens.
  6. Make wells and crack eggs into them.
  7. Cover and cook until eggs are set, then garnish with feta.
